What engine oil does

Oil coats the bearings, pistons, camshafts and other moving parts so metal doesn't grind on metal. It also carries heat away and holds dirt and soot until the filter traps them. Over time and miles, the oil breaks down and the filter fills up. That's when both get replaced together.

Signs it's time for an oil change

  • The oil-life monitor or maintenance reminder comes on.
  • You've reached the mileage or time in your owner's manual.
  • The oil level is low on the dipstick between changes.
  • The oil pressure warning light comes on. Stop the engine and check the level before driving farther.

Oil color isn't a reliable guide on its own; fresh oil can darken quickly in some engines. Go by the oil-life monitor or the schedule in your manual. An engine that keeps running low on oil between changes may be burning or leaking it. See oil leak and gasket repair.

Filter replacement

Each filter protects something different. The engine air filter keeps dust out of the engine. The cabin air filter cleans the air coming through your vents; a clogged one can cause weak airflow and a musty smell. The fuel filter keeps dirt away from the fuel pump and injectors. Many newer cars have the fuel filter built into the pump assembly, so it isn't a routine item on every car. We replace filters when they're due or dirty, not on every visit.

How often to change your oil

Your owner's manual or oil-life monitor sets the interval for your engine, and it varies a lot between cars and oil types. Many manuals also call for a change at least once a year, even with low mileage. Short trips, pulling a trailer, stop-and-go traffic and extreme temperatures count as severe service on many schedules, with shorter intervals. If you're not sure which schedule fits how you drive, ask us.

Oil changes and your warranty

Under federal law, a manufacturer can't void your warranty just because an independent shop did your routine maintenance. Use the oil the manual specifies and keep your receipts.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I change my oil?
Follow your owner's manual or the oil-life monitor. Intervals vary by engine and oil type, and many manuals list a shorter interval for severe driving such as short trips and stop-and-go traffic.
Do you use AMSOIL?
Yes. We're an authorized AMSOIL retailer and installer. We use an oil that meets the specification your manufacturer lists.
Does dark oil mean I need an oil change?
Not by itself. Oil can darken quickly in some engines. Go by the oil-life monitor or the schedule in your manual.
Will an oil change at an independent shop void my warranty?
No. Federal law says a manufacturer can't deny warranty coverage just because an independent shop did routine maintenance. Keep your receipts.
When should I replace my cabin air filter?
Check your owner's manual for the interval. Weak airflow from the vents or a musty smell can mean it's clogged sooner.
My oil light came on while driving. What should I do?
Pull over somewhere safe and shut the engine off. Low oil pressure can damage an engine quickly. Check the oil level, and call us at 610-626-4700 before driving it farther.
