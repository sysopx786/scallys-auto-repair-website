Where it began

It all started with a love for cars.

My name is Jerry Scally, and cars have been a big part of my life for more than 52 years.

Before Scally's Autorepair, I ran an Exxon gas station in Philadelphia. I've always enjoyed working on cars, especially bodywork. There's a certain satisfaction in taking something that needs fixing and making it right again.

But somewhere along the way, I wanted something of my own: a repair shop where I could take pride in the work and build relationships with the people who walked through the door.

In 1987, when I was about 38, that idea became Scally's Autorepair. I knew cars. I still had plenty to learn about running a business. But I knew one thing: I wanted to build a shop people could trust.

The first five years

Building something that would last.

The early years were about establishing ourselves, one repair and one customer at a time.

Running your own shop means taking responsibility. People rely on you to get their vehicles safely back on the road.

My approach was straightforward: do the job properly, explain what needs fixing, and treat people fairly. That still matters today.

The heart of Scally's

It's the people who keep coming back.

If you ask me what I'm most proud of after nearly 40 years, it isn't simply the number of cars we've repaired. It's the relationships we've built along the way.

Some customers have known Scally's since the 1980s and 1990s. Over the years, you get to know more than the cars they drive. You see people change vehicles, raise families, and come back when they need a hand. Sometimes that trust gets passed along to the next generation.

A customer who returns after 20 or 30 years is telling you something important. They're telling you they trust you. We never take that for granted. We have to earn it every time someone brings us a car.

Looking back, moving forward

Nearly 40 years later, here's what matters most.

After 52 years in the automotive business and nearly four decades at Scally's, I'm proud of the work we've done, the team we've built, and especially the people who have trusted us over the years.

If I could go back and give my younger self one piece of advice, it would be simple: “Do good work, treat people right, and never forget who helped you get where you are.”

To our longtime customers, thank you for sticking with us. And if you're visiting for the first time, we'd be glad for the chance to earn your trust.

— Jerry Scally, Owner
