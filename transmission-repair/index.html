How the transmission works

The transmission sends engine power to the wheels through the drivetrain. In an automatic, the transmission changes gears on its own; in a manual, you work the clutch and the shifter. A change in how the car shifts can be the transmission, while a clunk when you step on the gas can come from a mount, an axle or another drivetrain part.

'Slipping' and 'not shifting right' mean different things to different drivers. Note whether it happens from a stop, while accelerating, in reverse, or only once the car is warm, and whether the engine revs climb without the car speeding up. Those details guide the diagnosis. They don't decide the repair on their own.

Signs of transmission trouble

  • Shifts feel harsh, delayed or unusually soft.
  • The engine revs up but the car doesn't speed up to match.
  • A delay before the car moves when you shift into drive or reverse.
  • Whining, grinding or clunking while driving or shifting.
  • A burning smell, or fluid under the car.
  • A transmission warning or check engine light.

Each of these can have more than one cause. A vibration doesn't prove the transmission needs a rebuild, and a harsh shift doesn't automatically mean a worn mount. If the car loses drive, makes a loud grinding noise or won't stay in gear, stop driving it somewhere safe and call us. Forcing it farther can turn a repair into a rebuild.

Transmission rebuilding

A rebuild keeps your transmission and replaces the worn parts inside it, such as clutch packs, bands, seals and gaskets, as needed. It can make sense when the case and the major hard parts are still sound.

Transmission replacement

Replacement means installing another transmission. It can be the better route when the damage is extensive or a rebuild would cost more than it's worth. We explain what we found and the options for your vehicle before you decide.

Transmission fluid service

Transmission fluid cools and lubricates the transmission, and in an automatic it also carries the hydraulic pressure that makes it shift. Your owner's manual lists the fluid type and the service interval, and both vary widely between transmissions. Some transmissions have no dipstick, so checking the fluid takes a lift and the right procedure. The wrong fluid can cause shifting problems, so we use what the manufacturer specifies.

When it isn't the transmission

Some complaints that feel like transmission trouble come from the parts around it. A clunk when you shift into drive can be a worn transmission mount or motor mount. A click while turning usually points to a CV axle. A hum from the back of the car can be a rear axle bearing or the differential. On a manual, slipping is often the clutch, not the gearbox. Checking these first can keep you from paying for transmission work you don't need.

Frequently asked questions

Does rough shifting mean I need a new transmission?
Not necessarily. Low or wrong fluid, a sensor or electrical fault, a worn mount or worn internal parts can all cause rough shifts. We diagnose before we talk about a rebuild or replacement.
What's the difference between rebuilding and replacing a transmission?
Rebuilding repairs your existing transmission with new internal parts. Replacing installs another transmission. The better choice depends on the damage, the vehicle and the parts available.
How often should transmission fluid be changed?
Follow the interval and fluid type in your owner's manual. It varies by transmission, and many manuals list a shorter interval for pulling a trailer or heavy stop-and-go driving.
What causes a transmission to slip?
Low or worn-out fluid, worn internal clutches or bands, or a control problem can cause slipping. The symptom alone doesn't identify the failed part, so we test.
Can I keep driving with a slipping transmission?
It's not a good idea. Driving on can make the damage worse, and a transmission that loses drive in traffic is unsafe. If it slips, flares or won't stay in gear, stop driving it and call us.
Do you work on manual transmissions?
Yes. We repair automatic and manual transmissions, and we do clutch work on manuals.
